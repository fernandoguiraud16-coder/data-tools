# Guides

- [YouTube transcript API for Python](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/youtube-transcript-api-python/): Get YouTube transcripts in Python without getting blocked: videos, whole channels and playlists as text, SRT or Markdown, with AI transcription when a video has no captions.
- [Transcripts of a whole YouTube channel or playlist](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/youtube-channel-transcripts/): Bulk-download the transcripts of every video of a YouTube channel or playlist as text, SRT or Markdown, and get only new uploads on a schedule.
- [TikTok transcript API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/tiktok-transcript-api/): Transcribe TikTok videos or the newest videos of any account to text and SRT with Whisper. Works without captions, no login or TikTok API key.
- [Instagram Reels transcript API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/instagram-reels-transcript-api/): Transcribe Instagram Reels by link, or a creator's newest reels by @username, to text and SRT with Whisper. No login or cookies.
- [Podcast transcript API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/podcast-transcript-api/): Transcribe podcast episodes by podcast name, Apple Podcasts link or RSS feed, with speaker labels and only-new-episodes mode. Whisper, 99 languages.
- [Speech to text API with Whisper](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/speech-to-text-api-whisper/): Transcribe MP3, WAV, M4A and MP4 files to text, timestamps and subtitles with open-source Whisper. 99 languages, translation to English, $0.006 per minute.
- [Video to text API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/video-to-text-api/): Transcribe videos from TikTok, Instagram Reels, X, Facebook or MP4 links to text and subtitles, with optional speaker labels. Whisper, $0.006 per minute.
- [SRT subtitle generator API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/srt-subtitle-generator-api/): Generate SRT and VTT subtitle files from any video or audio with Whisper, formatted to broadcast rules or short lines for Reels and TikTok. 99 languages.
- [Transcripts as chunks for RAG](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/transcript-chunks-for-rag/): Turn audio, video, podcasts and YouTube into timestamped text chunks ready for embeddings, vector databases and LLM apps.
- [PDF to Markdown for RAG and LLMs](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/pdf-to-markdown-for-rag/): Convert PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint and scanned documents to clean Markdown, tables and RAG chunks with page numbers. OCR included, $0.003 per document.
- [Extract tables from PDF to Excel and CSV](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/pdf-table-extraction-excel/): Extract every table from PDFs and scans into Excel (one sheet per table), CSV and JSON. Multi-page tables merged, OCR for scans, $0.003 per document.
- [Extract text from PDFs in Python](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/pdf-text-extraction-python/): Extract the text of PDFs in bulk with Python: full text, text per page, title, author and page count, with OCR for scanned PDFs. $0.003 per document.
- [Bulk WHOIS lookup API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/bulk-whois-lookup-api/): Look up thousands of domains at once: registrar, creation and expiry dates, domain age, availability, DNS, email provider and SSL expiry. $0.002 per domain.
- [Monitor domain and SSL expiry](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/domain-expiry-monitoring/): Track domain and SSL certificate expiry dates for a list of domains on a schedule and get alerts N days before. DNS and registrar changes included.
- [Tech stack lookup API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/tech-stack-lookup-api/): Detect the CMS, analytics, frameworks and 7,000+ technologies of a list of websites, plus hosting, DNS, SSL and SEO. BuiltWith and Wappalyzer alternative.
- [Google Trends API for Python](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/google-trends-api-python/): Get Google Trends interest over time and by region, related and rising queries and trending searches in Python, with automatic retries instead of 429 errors.
- [Google News API with full article text](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/google-news-api-full-text/): Search Google News by keyword, section or country and get the real article URL plus the full article text in Markdown for LLMs and RAG. Only-new mode.
- [Google Ads Transparency Center API](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/google-ads-transparency-api/): Get every Google ad of any advertiser or website domain from the Ads Transparency Center: text, image and YouTube ads with dates and regions. $0.0015 per ad.
- [A pytrends alternative that does not hit 429 errors](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/pytrends-alternative/): pytrends keeps failing with 429 Too Many Requests and has had no release since April 2023. A maintained alternative that retries with fresh IPs and returns clean JSON.
- [youtube-transcript-api blocked on your server? A hosted fix](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/youtube-transcript-api-blocked/): youtube-transcript-api raises RequestBlocked or IpBlocked on cloud servers because YouTube blocks cloud IPs. Get transcripts through rotating residential IPs instead, no proxy setup.
- [An OpenAI Whisper API alternative without the 25 MB limit](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/openai-whisper-api-alternative/): OpenAI's speech-to-text API caps uploads at 25 MB. Transcribe long files from links, TikTok or Instagram, with SRT/VTT and speaker labels, using open-source Whisper. No OpenAI key.
- [A pay-per-domain BuiltWith alternative](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/builtwith-alternative/): Check the technology stack of your own list of domains for $0.004 each, with hosting, DNS, SSL and WHOIS, instead of a BuiltWith subscription.
- [A Wappalyzer API alternative for bulk lookups](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/wappalyzer-api-alternative/): Detect website technologies in bulk through an API, pay $0.004 per domain with no monthly credits that expire. Includes DNS, SSL, hosting and SEO checks.
- [A python-whois alternative for bulk lookups](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/python-whois-alternative/): Raw WHOIS text differs by registry and gets rate-limited. Bulk RDAP/WHOIS lookups with one clean schema for every TLD, from Python, $0.002 per domain.
- [A Tabula alternative for PDF tables](https://fernandoguiraud16-coder.github.io/data-tools/guides/tabula-alternative/): tabula-py needs Java and cannot read scanned PDFs. Extract PDF tables to Excel, CSV and JSON through an API, with OCR for scans, $0.003 per document.
